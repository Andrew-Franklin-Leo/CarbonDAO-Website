# Introduction to CarbonDAO

Welcome to the CarbonDAO documentation! This guide will help you understand our decentralized carbon credit trading platform and how to participate in the ecosystem.

## What is CarbonDAO?

CarbonDAO is a decentralized autonomous organization (DAO) focused on making carbon credit trading more accessible, transparent, and efficient through blockchain technology. Our platform connects carbon credit producers with buyers while ensuring the highest standards of verification and compliance.

## Key Features

### Carbon Credit Trading
- Transparent marketplace for carbon credits
- Real-time pricing and market data
- Instant settlement of trades
- Full transaction history and audit trail

### Decentralized Governance
- Community-driven decision making
- Token-based voting system
- Proposal submission and review process
- Stake-based governance rights

### Project Registry
- Comprehensive project verification
- International standards compliance
- Real-time monitoring and reporting
- Automated credit issuance
