# XVIII. Business Model & Revenue Streams

*   **Revenue Streams:**
    *   **Transaction Fees:**
        *   A percentage-based fee on carbon credit transactions.
    *   **Listing Fees:**
        *   A one-time fee for listing carbon sequestration projects.
    *   **Data and Analytics Subscriptions:**
        *   Offering premium data and analytics to investors and project developers.
*   **Key Partners:**
    *   **Project Developers:**
        *   Collaborating with developers to source high-quality carbon sequestration projects.
    *   **Environmental Organizations:**
        *   Partnering with organizations to promote the DAO's mission and projects.
*   **Key Activities:**
    *   **Project Selection and Verification:**
        *   Ensuring the quality and effectiveness of listed projects.
    *   **Marketplace Operations:**
        *   Maintaining a secure, user-friendly, and transparent marketplace.
*   **Key Resources:**
    *   **Blockchain Infrastructure:**
        *   Utilizing a robust and scalable blockchain platform.
    *   **Development Team:**
        *   Employing experienced developers for platform maintenance and updates.
*   **Value Proposition:**
    *   **For Investors:**
        *   A diversified portfolio of carbon sequestration projects.
    *   **For Project Developers:**
        *   Access to a global marketplace for carbon credits.
