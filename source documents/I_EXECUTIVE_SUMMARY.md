# I. Executive Summary

*   Overview of the Decentralized Global Carbon Sequestration DAO
    *   The Decentralized Global Carbon Sequestration DAO is a decentralized autonomous organization that aims to finance and manage large-scale carbon sequestration projects, leveraging blockchain technology for transparency and efficiency.
*   Mission and Vision
    *   Mission: To mitigate climate change by promoting and scaling effective carbon sequestration projects through a transparent and decentralized platform.
    *   Vision: To become the leading global DAO for carbon sequestration, empowering individuals and organizations to actively participate in climate action.
*   Value Proposition
    *   Transparency: Blockchain-based tracking and verification of carbon credits, ensuring accountability and trust.
    *   Decentralization: Community-driven governance and project selection, fostering inclusivity and innovation.
    *   Efficiency: Streamlined funding and management of carbon sequestration projects, reducing costs and increasing impact.
*   Key Objectives
    *   Launch the DAO and establish a functional governance system by Q1 2025.
    *   Secure partnerships with at least 10 leading carbon sequestration project developers by Q2 2025.
    *   Tokenize carbon credits and create a liquid marketplace by Q3 2025.
    *   Attract a global community of at least 10,000 investors and climate activists by Q4 2025.
*   Financial Highlights
    *   Projected revenue of $10 million in the first year, growing to $50 million by year 3.
    *   Targeting a 20% annual growth rate.
    *   Seeking $2 million in seed funding.
*   Funding Request
    *   Seeking $2 million in seed funding to cover initial operating expenses, technology development, and project acquisition.
    *   The funding will be used to build the blockchain platform, establish partnerships, and launch marketing campaigns.
