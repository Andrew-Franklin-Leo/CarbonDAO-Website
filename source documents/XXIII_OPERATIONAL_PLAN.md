# XXIII. Operational Plan

*   **Project Selection and Verification:**
    *   **Due Diligence:**
        *   Conducting thorough due diligence on potential carbon sequestration projects.
    *   **Third-Party Audits:**
        *   Engaging independent auditors to verify project claims.
*   **Tokenization Process:**
    *   **Carbon Credit Conversion:**
        *   Converting verified carbon credits into ERC-20 tokens.
    *   **Smart Contract Deployment:**
        *   Deploying smart contracts to manage tokenized carbon credits.
*   **DAO Governance:**
    *   **Proposal Submission:**
        *   Allowing token holders to submit proposals for project funding and DAO improvements.
    *   **Voting Mechanism:**
        *   Implementing a transparent and secure voting mechanism.
*   **Marketplace Operations:**
    *   **Listing and Trading:**
        *   Facilitating the listing and trading of carbon credits on the DAO's marketplace.
    *   **Escrow Services:**
        *   Providing secure escrow services for carbon credit transactions.
*   **Data and Analytics:**
    *   **Data Collection:**
        *   Collecting data on carbon sequestration projects and the carbon market.
    *   **Reporting:**
        *   Generating reports on project performance and market trends.
