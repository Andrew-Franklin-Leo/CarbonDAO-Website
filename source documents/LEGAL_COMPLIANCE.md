# Legal Compliance

## Jurisdiction

* **Selected Jurisdiction:** Singapore
* **Rationale:** Favorable cryptocurrency laws and regulations

## Regulatory Compliance

* **Anti-Money Laundering (AML):** Implement KYC/AML checks for all users
* **Know-Your-Customer (KYC):** Verify user identities through government-issued IDs
* **Data Protection:** Comply with General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA)

## Licensing and Permits

* **Obtain necessary licenses:** Register with relevant regulatory bodies
* **Permit Renewal:** Schedule annual renewals for all permits
