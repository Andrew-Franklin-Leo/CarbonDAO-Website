# Risk Assessment

## Market Risks

* **Token Volatility:** Mitigate with diversified treasury and stablecoin reserves
* **Market Competition:** Differentiate through innovative features and strong community engagement

## Technical Risks

* **Smart Contract Vulnerabilities:** Regular security audits and bug bounty program
* **Infrastructure Downtime:** Implement robust backup and disaster recovery systems

## Regulatory Risks

* **Changing Laws and Regulations:** Continuously monitor and adapt to new regulations
* **Non-Compliance Penalties:** Ensure timely compliance with all regulatory requirements
