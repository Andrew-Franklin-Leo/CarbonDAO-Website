# Decentralized Global Carbon Sequestration DAO - Business Plan

## 1. Executive Overview

### 1.1 Executive Summary
The Decentralized Global Carbon Sequestration DAO (DGCS-DAO) represents a revolutionary approach to addressing climate change through blockchain technology. Our platform connects carbon sequestration projects with global investors, creating a transparent and efficient marketplace for carbon credits while accelerating climate action.

Market Opportunity: $100B+ by 2030
Initial Target: $500M in carbon credit transactions by Year 3
Projected ROI: 25-30% annual returns for token holders

### 1.2 Vision and Mission Statement
Vision: To become the world's leading decentralized platform for carbon credit trading and climate action financing.

Mission: To accelerate global carbon sequestration efforts by connecting project developers with investors through blockchain technology, ensuring transparency, efficiency, and measurable impact.

## 4. Token Economics

### 4.1 Token Structure

#### Governance Token (CDAO)
- Total supply: 1,000,000,000
- Initial price: $0.10
- Vesting period: 4 years
- Staking rewards: 5-12% APY

#### Carbon Credit Token (CCR)
- Dynamic supply based on verified credits
- 1 CCR = 1 metric ton CO2
- Minimum trading unit: 0.1 CCR
- Burning mechanism for offset claims

### 4.2 Revenue Model

#### Transaction Fees
- Trading fee: 0.3%
- Project listing fee: 1,000 CDAO
- Verification fee: 2,500 CDAO
- Premium features: Subscription-based

#### Distribution
- 40% to stakers
- 30% to treasury
- 20% to project development
- 10% to community rewards
