# XII. Target Market Identification

*   Primary Target Market Segments
    *   Corporations seeking to offset their carbon emissions
    *   Individuals looking to invest in climate action
    *   Governments and organizations committed to sustainability
*   Secondary Target Market Segments
    *   Small and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs) with growing carbon footprints
    *   Environmental organizations and NGOs
    *   Research institutions and universities
*   Market Needs and Pain Points
    *   Corporations: Seeking cost-effective and transparent carbon offsetting solutions
    *   Individuals: Looking for trustworthy and impactful climate investment opportunities
    *   Governments and organizations: Needing reliable and scalable carbon sequestration solutions
