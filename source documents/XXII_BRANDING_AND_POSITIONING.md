# XXII. Branding & Positioning

*   **Brand Identity:**
    *   **Name:** Decentralized Global Carbon Sequestration DAO
    *   **Logo:** A stylized, interconnected globe with a tree at its center
    *   **Color Scheme:** A calming green and blue palette to evoke feelings of nature and trust
*   **Brand Messaging:**
    *   **Tagline:** "Empowering a global community to combat climate change through decentralized carbon sequestration"
    *   **Elevator Pitch:** A brief, engaging summary of the DAO's mission and value proposition
*   **Brand Positioning:**
    *   **Unique Selling Proposition (USP):** Decentralized, community-driven, and transparent carbon sequestration
    *   **Target Audience:** Environmentally conscious individuals, corporations, and governments
*   **Brand Guidelines:**
    *   **Logo Usage:** Clear guidelines for logo placement and sizing
    *   **Color Palette:** Specific color codes for digital and print materials
    *   **Typography:** Recommended font families and font sizes
