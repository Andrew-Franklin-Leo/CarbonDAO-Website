# VI. Operations Plan

*   Operational Overview
    *   The DAO's operations will be managed by a core team of experts in carbon sequestration, blockchain technology, and business management.
*   Project Selection and Verification
    *   A rigorous project selection process will be used to identify high-quality carbon sequestration projects.
    *   Projects will be verified by independent third-party auditors.
*   Tokenization Process
    *   Verified carbon credits will be tokenized as ERC-20 tokens on the Ethereum blockchain.
    *   The tokenization process will be transparent and auditable.
*   DAO Governance Process
    *   Token holders will be able to participate in the DAO's governance by voting on proposals.
    *   The governance process will be transparent and democratic.
*   Marketplace Operations
    *   The marketplace will be operated by the DAO's core team.
    *   The marketplace will be secure and user-friendly.
*   Data and Analytics Operations
    *   The DAO will collect and analyze data on carbon sequestration projects and the carbon market.
    *   The data will be used to inform investment decisions and improve the DAO's operations.
