# XXI. Sales Strategy

*   **Sales Objectives:**
    *   Generate revenue through the sale of carbon credits.
    *   Acquire new customers and retain existing ones.
*   **Sales Channels:**
    *   **Direct Sales:**
        *   Engage with corporations and organizations directly.
    *   **Online Marketplace:**
        *   Utilize the DAO's online platform for individual investors.
    *   **Partnerships:**
        *   Collaborate with carbon offset providers and environmental organizations.
*   **Sales Tactics:**
    *   **Account Management:**
        *   Assign dedicated account managers for key clients.
    *   **Lead Generation:**
        *   Utilize marketing efforts to generate new leads.
    *   **Sales Enablement:**
        *   Provide sales teams with necessary tools and training.
*   **Sales Metrics and Monitoring:**
    *   **Sales Revenue:**
        *   Track and analyze revenue generated from carbon credit sales.
    *   **Customer Acquisition:**
        *   Monitor the number of new customers acquired.
    *   **Customer Retention:**
        *   Track and analyze customer retention rates.
