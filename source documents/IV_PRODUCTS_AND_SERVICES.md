# IV. Products and Services

*   Carbon Sequestration Projects
    *   Reforestation and Afforestation: Planting trees to absorb carbon dioxide from the atmosphere.
    *   Direct Air Capture: Using technology to capture carbon dioxide directly from the air.
    *   Ocean Fertilization: Enhancing ocean productivity to increase carbon sequestration.
*   Carbon Credit Tokenization
    *   Tokenizing verified carbon credits as ERC-20 tokens on the Ethereum blockchain.
    *   Ensuring transparency and traceability of carbon credits.
*   DAO Governance
    *   Token holders can participate in the DAO's governance by voting on proposals.
    *   Proposals can include project selection, funding allocation, and changes to the DAO's parameters.
*   Marketplace
    *   A decentralized marketplace for buying and selling carbon credits.
    *   Facilitating price discovery and liquidity for carbon credits.
*   Data and Analytics
    *   Providing data and analytics on carbon sequestration projects and the carbon market.
    *   Helping investors make informed decisions.
