# II. Company Description

*   Company Overview
    *   The Decentralized Global Carbon Sequestration DAO is a decentralized autonomous organization (DAO) that leverages blockchain technology to address climate change through carbon sequestration.
*   Mission, Vision, and Values
    *   Mission: To mitigate climate change by promoting and scaling effective carbon sequestration projects through a transparent and decentralized platform.
    *   Vision: To become the leading global DAO for carbon sequestration, empowering individuals and organizations to actively participate in climate action.
    *   Values: Transparency, decentralization, sustainability, and community.
*   Legal Structure and Ownership
    *   The DAO will be registered as a legal entity in a jurisdiction that recognizes DAOs, such as Switzerland or Singapore.
    *   Ownership will be distributed among token holders, who will have voting rights in the DAO's governance.
*   History and Background
    *   The DAO was founded in 2024 by a team of climate scientists, blockchain developers, and business professionals.
    *   The team has a proven track record of success in their respective fields.
