# IX. Risk Management

*   Risk Assessment
    *   Market Risk: Volatility in the price of carbon credits.
    *   Technology Risk: Security vulnerabilities in the blockchain platform.
    *   Regulatory Risk: Changes in government regulations related to carbon markets.
    *   Operational Risk: Inefficient project selection and verification processes.
*   Mitigation Strategies
    *   Diversifying the portfolio of carbon sequestration projects.
    *   Implementing robust security measures to protect the blockchain platform.
    *   Staying informed about regulatory changes and adapting the DAO's operations accordingly.
    *   Establishing a rigorous project selection and verification process.
